| Zero-sum framing of the succession by any principal party | **no** | all parties still argue in institutional-interest terms |

**Register: `elevated-structural`.** Five of five positioning indicators present; zero of four adversarial-escalation indicators present. This sphere plays hard *through* legitimate structure and has not yet played outside it. Nobody here is lying about facts, and nobody has yet made a move that costs an individual their standing. Register consequence: the framework is used at full structural depth, and the tone stays professional-adversarial rather than conflict-grade.

**Depth-decoder bias: `b = 0.5` (mid-range), stated as a two-sided loss.** *Under*-reading depth is a live risk here: Robert's deferrals and Carol's memo timing are exactly the signals a depth-≥1 player produces, and reading them at face value is precisely how Sam's § 4.3 blind spot (over-reading operational engagement as loyalty) does its damage. *Over*-reading is an equally live risk: with all four escalation indicators absent, decoding Robert's substantive operational engagement as bait would throw away the sphere's best true signal and would coach Sam into suspicion of a counterpart who is, on the current evidence, deliberating rather than managing. Neither loss dominates the other at this register, so neither endpoint is defensible — `b = 0.5`, applied as partial discounting.

**Re-read trigger.** If any of the four absent indicators appears — external escalation or a retaliation event above all — re-run this reading before the next probe design. `b` moves up, and every signal previously decoded at `b = 0.5` gets re-read under the new decoder (a depth-update re-decodes prior signals; `08-ESTIMATION` § The depth decoder, point 2).

**Epistemic status (P13).** `b` is uncalibrated. This sphere has no QUARTERLY-AUDIT calibration record for it. The 0.5 is an ordinal midpoint expressing *"neither endpoint is defensible here,"* not a measured quantity; it organizes the decode and licenses no precision.

**Scale provenance — flagged.** OPC v3.0 § 2.4 `political_intensity_diagnostic` specifies register calibration to the environment's political intensity; `_frameworks/OPC-Assessment.md` documents the subsection but ships no atom-level indicator list and no numeric band for it.
