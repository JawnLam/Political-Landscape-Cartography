2. `mutual_benefit` — Non-tactical win-win framing
3. `trust_building` — Long-term relational investment

5. `value_creation` — Real improvement changing the calculus

8. `culture_building` — Cultural intervention as the move
9. `talent_development` — Developing someone as the leverage
10. `sustainable_success` — Long-term alternative dominating tactical play

ALTERNATIVES-AUDIT gate walks all 10 approaches in cycle order; surfaces any that map to legitimate moves; rejects tactical options that bypass available constructive alternatives without override justification.
