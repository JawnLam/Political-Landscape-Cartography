# Lam 2018 Pepperdine Dissertation — Request for Source

The full text of the Methodology Author's doctoral dissertation —

> *Lam, C. (J.) (2018). The Accumulation, Utilization, And Protection Of Political Capital By Senior Executives Of For-Profit Organizations.* Pepperdine University.

— is the foundational substrate for everything in this Operating Volume. It is also the Methodology Author's personal academic work, and is not distributed through this repository.

**If you are looking for the actual dissertation, contact Cong "Jawn" Lam directly to request more details.**

Operators who have been granted access place their copy at the path `_frameworks/sources/Lam-2018-Pepperdine.pdf` — alongside this file. That PDF is excluded from version-control synchronization by the repository's `.gitignore` and must not be committed or pushed to any remote.

## What the dissertation contains (for orientation)

For those evaluating whether the source is relevant to their work, the dissertation provides:

- The grounded-theory methodology that produced the 19 theoretical categories underlying PLC's interaction-cycle model
- Table 3 (pages 129-145) cataloging ~100 granular political-action Moves with their PP/AA/B/C/Inc/Dec coding
- The slash addressing notation and nomenclature (pages 162-167)
- Five worked case studies (Charles / Jim / Nathan / Sarah / Rusty) walking through Interaction Cycle tables 5-22
- The theoretical model with both theoretical-category and slash-addressing labels (Figures 3 and 4, pages 220-221)
- The 9-type resource taxonomy with verbatim definitions (Category #14, pages 155-157)
- The Discussion section's three-part framing of how political capital is built, used, and protected (pages 222-225)

PLC's `_frameworks/dissertation-substrate.md` documents the dissertation's structural contribution at a level that may be sufficient for some uses without requiring access to the full text.
