## Description (verbatim from dissertation page 130)

Trying to fit in with the culture.

## When to Use

- New-actor onboarding into an established organizational culture
- Cross-functional or cross-organizational engagements where cultural distance is a friction-cost driver
- Adversarial relationship recovery contexts where cultural alignment signals good faith

## When Not to Use

- When the existing culture is itself the source of dysfunction the actor's agenda is correcting
- When cultural fit signals would be read as inauthentic or capitulation
- When the actor's distinct cultural positioning is itself a load-bearing political asset (e.g., the outsider mandate)

## Resources Required

- **Attributes** — adaptability, social acumen
- **Information** — political knowledge of which cultural signals the target reads as fit-indicators
- **Tribe** — implicit membership signals; if absent, this Move has limited efficacy
