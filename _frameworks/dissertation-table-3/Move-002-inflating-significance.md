## Description (verbatim from dissertation page 130)

Inflating the significance or magnitude of the actor's duties and responsibilities.

## When to Use

- Annual review / performance evaluation contexts where the target weighs the actor's contribution
- Promotion / advancement decisions where role-significance influences the decision matrix
- Resource-allocation discussions where the actor's responsibilities justify additional resources

## When Not to Use

- When the actor's actual track record is well-documented and inflation will be transparently detected
- When the target has independent verification channels that surface the gap
- When the political environment is competence-tight and inflation creates credibility risk

## Resources Required

- **Information** — knowledge of how the target weighs role-significance
- **Credibility** — pre-existing track record that the inflation can plausibly extend
- **Attributes** — confidence/poise to make the inflation read as natural
