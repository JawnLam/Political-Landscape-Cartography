## Description (verbatim from dissertation page 130)

The actor makes efforts that will control how the target will draw implicit conclusions about the actor's own character, competence, or intentions.

## When to Use

- Engagement contexts where the target's read of the actor is variable and shapable
- Pre-cycle preparation phase — perception management establishes baseline before specific tactical moves
- Long-horizon engagements where character/competence/intention impressions compound over time

## When Not to Use

- Single-cycle, single-target situations where direct tactical moves are more efficient
- Contexts where the target has fixed, evidence-grounded reads of the actor
- When the target's existing read is favorable and intervention risks degradation

## Resources Required

- **Information** (especially political knowledge about what reads the target holds and how those reads form)
- **Attributes** (nontransferable qualities the actor can deploy — credibility, presence, etc.)
