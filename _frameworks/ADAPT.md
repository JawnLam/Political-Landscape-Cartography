# ADAPT — Lookup Card

> **One-page reference for the 5-beat ADAPT coaching loop.** The commercialized framework the Methodology Author (Cong "Jawn" Lam) deploys at the principal-coaching layer above PLC's political-landscape layer. Full source: `sources/ADAPT-Loop-Field-Manual.pdf`.

## The 5 beats

| Beat | What it does | Maps to PLC protocol |
|------|--------------|---------------------|
| **A — Agenda** | Articulate the principal's intent at sufficient specificity for political reasoning to operate | RUN-CYCLE initiation (Interest formed within the Agenda context) |
| **D — Decide** | Narrow the agenda into decision criteria; build the principal's funnel | RUN-CYCLE Detection through capability-gate clearance (Landscape Assessment + OPC Self-Diagnosis + Funnel + ALTERNATIVES-AUDIT + ETHICAL-ACCOUNTING + Considered + Selected) |
| **A — Act** | Execute the selected move; engage; commit | RUN-CYCLE Engaged Moves (post-capability-gate Execution) |
| **P — Perceive** | Read the actual response; surface perception gaps | RUN-CYCLE Execution through Outcome (Phase Log + Track-and-Detect) |
| **T — Track** | Refine OPC + funnel based on what was perceived; close the loop | RUN-CYCLE Refinement + Lessons + cycle close |

## Recursion

ADAPT is recursive at multiple scales:

- **Per-move recursion** — a single move within a cycle can itself be a mini-ADAPT (A → D → A → P → T at the move grain)
- **Per-cycle recursion** — the full 14-constituent cycle is one ADAPT pass at the cycle grain
- **Per-engagement recursion** — the engagement's lifecycle (`pre_engagement → routine → close_out → closed`) is one ADAPT pass at the engagement grain
- **Per-practice recursion** — the consultant's longitudinal practice across engagements is ADAPT at the practice grain (D23 Layer 3 structured export populates the Track beat at this scale)

## When ADAPT is the right form

- Principal-coaching layer of any politically-substantive engagement
- Recursive cycle work where each pass refines the prior pass's read
- Practitioner-development reflection (the consultant's own ADAPT cycle over their craft)

## When ADAPT is NOT the right form

- Operational tactical decisions where speed > reflection
- Crisis-response where the Perceive beat takes longer than the action window allows
- Algorithmic decision-making where structure doesn't change

## How ADAPT layers with PLC

ADAPT is the coaching frame; PLC is the political-landscape frame. They operate at different layers and complement each other:

- **PLC answers:** what is the sphere's political state? Who are the actors? What moves are viable? What gates clear?
- **ADAPT answers:** how does the principal hold their agenda? How does decision-quality improve over cycles? How does the principal develop political mastery?

In consultant mode, the operator runs ADAPT with the principal at the coaching layer while PLC structures the political-landscape work. In principal-self mode, the operator runs ADAPT on themselves.
