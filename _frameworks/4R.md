# 4R — Conversational Frame for the Political Landscape

> **The Methodology Author's client-facing conceptual frame over the dissertation's full interaction-cycle mechanism.** Four R-concepts arranged on a baseball diamond — used during conversation to give the operator and the principal a shared mental map of the political terrain they're navigating together. The terrain itself is mapped in detail elsewhere in the OV; 4R is the verbal frame that operator and principal hold in common while talking about it.
>
> **What 4R is NOT:** it is not a deliverable, not an artifact, not a visual the operator produces for the client, not a model, not a framework in the methodological sense, not a process the principal executes. There is no physical or graphical thing called "the 4Rs" that the client receives. It exists only in conversation, as a shared mental map both parties can refer to without belaboring the underlying machinery.
>
> **What 4R IS:** a way to conversationally walk a principal — or a prospective client — through the structure of political capital accumulation, in language they can hold in their head while you both talk about specifics. The analytical engine (the dissertation's 14-constituent interaction cycle, the slash addressing, the nested-Interest recursion, the OPC diagnostic, the Catalogue lookup) does the actual cartography. 4R is just the frame for talking about what that cartography produces.

## The four R's

| Base | R | What it is | Dissertation correspondence |
|------|---|------------|----------------------------|
| Home plate | **Resources** | The principal's current resource inventory | `[a./R.]` — the actor's resource set across the 9-type taxonomy |
| 1st base | **Results** | The change the principal's deployed resources produce in someone's decision matrix | Outcome → Obtained utility flowing into a target's `Σ(Α/i./t./D.)` |
| 2nd base | **Rationale** | The decision matrix of the actor the principal is trying to influence | `[Α/i./t./D.]` — the target's Decision Matrix with its Preferred Position vs Adverse Alternative columns + Benefit/Cost considerations |
| 3rd base | **Relationship** | The actor whose rationale the principal is moving — and who happens to control the new resource the principal needs | `[Α/i./t.]` — the target Actor in the slash-addressed interaction cycle |

## The flywheel

The four bases form a closed cycle that returns to home plate with a new resource:

```
                                      Resources (home)
                                            ▲
                                            │
                                  acquires NEW resource
                                            │
       (acquired from the Actor)            │
   Relationship (3rd) ◄───────────► Results (1st)
        ▲                                   │
        │                                   ▼
        │                              Rationale (2nd)
        │                                   │
        └─────── you move their ────────────┘
                rationale by the
                results you deliver
```

Each completed cycle returns the principal to home plate with a richer resource inventory. The flywheel's mass is the principal's accumulated resource state; its momentum is the rate at which resources convert to results convert to rationale-shifts convert to new resources. More mass + more momentum = more power.

The single load-bearing line of the entire conceptual frame:

> **Use the resources you control to get control of resources you don't yet control.**

## The four levels of player sophistication

The 4R conversation also serves as a quiet coaching diagnostic — it lets the operator place the principal on a sophistication scale by listening to how they describe their work.

| Level | Cognitive depth | Signal in how they describe their work |
|-------|-----------------|----------------------------------------|
| **Little League** | Home → 1st only | Talks about "what I'm going to do" without considering whose mind it has to move |
| **Minor League** | Home → 1st → 2nd | Anticipates the target's decision criteria; thinks one move ahead about how the move will land |
| **Major League** (Political Tactician) | Reverse-engineers from 3rd base back | Starts with "what resource do I need next?" → identifies who has it → models what would make them give it up → designs the result that creates that pressure → assembles the resources to produce that result. Three bases of look-ahead = thinking like a tactician. |
| **All-Star** (Elite Political Strategist) | Sees the whole network of diamonds | Recognizes that every actor in the landscape has their own 4R diamond running in parallel; the diamonds are interconnected, interdependent, and constantly in motion. Plays moves that look innocuous today and materialize as checkmate later. |

Most fresh-MBA principals are Little League. With experience they often reach Minor League on their own. Major League is where the engagement's structural value starts: reverse-engineering at scale across many parallel resource-acquisition threads is exactly the cognitive load the OV is designed to offload from the principal.

All-Star is rare and not directly coachable. What IS coachable is the discipline of maintaining the full actor-network model the All-Star sees naturally — which is what PLC's Actor cartography + slash addressing + nested-Interest tracking accomplishes structurally.

## Relationship to the dissertation's interaction cycle

4R is a high-level abstraction over the dissertation's 14-element interaction cycle structure (per Lam 2018, Tables 5-22). The full cycle is:

1. Interest → 2. Detection → 3. Target → 4. Preferred position → 5. Adverse alternative → 6. Objective → 7. Intention → 8. Required resources → **9. Capability-gate** → 10. Execution → 11. Perception → 12. Outcome → 13. Obtained utility → 14. Obtained resource

The 4R bases map onto this as:

- **Resources (home)** ↔ the actor's current resource set `[a./R.]` available for constituents 7-8
- **Results (1st)** ↔ constituents 10-13 (Execution through Obtained utility) — the change the actor produces in the target
- **Rationale (2nd)** ↔ constituents 4-6 (Preferred position / Adverse alternative / Objective) — the target's decision matrix structure
- **Relationship (3rd)** ↔ constituents 2-3 (Detection / Target) — identifying the right actor to move
- **NEW resource back at home** ↔ constituent 14 (Obtained Resource) — the cycle's output that flows back into `[a./R.]`

Constituents 7-9 (Intention / Required Resources / capability-gate) aren't in the 4R model because they're the internal mechanism — the moment the actor's intent meets the resource constraint. When the **capability-gate** (constituent 9) fails — `[a./R.] ∌ <required resource>` — the actor must spawn a nested interaction cycle to obtain the missing resource (the "nested interests for resource accumulation" mechanism). The 4R conversation holds the principal's attention on the four bases; the OV's slash-addressing layer tracks the recursion depth and the nested-cycle obligations.

## When 4R fits the conversation

- **Initial client orientation** — first 30-60 minutes of any new engagement; gives the principal a frame they can hold immediately
- **Mid-engagement re-orientation** — when the principal is overwhelmed by complexity, return them to "which base are we on right now?"
- **Prospect conversations** — the Little League → All-Star progression makes the case for engaging a consultant + adopting structural infrastructure
- **Principal-self-mode operators** — the frame the principal returns to between sessions to maintain orientation without loading the full 14-element model
- **Briefing other operators** — explaining to a new consultant or supervising peer what an engagement is doing without exposing the slash-addressing detail

## When 4R is too thin for the conversation

- **Audit-trail work** — the full 14-element cycle + slash addressing is required for ETHICAL-ACCOUNTING + ALTERNATIVES-AUDIT gate evaluation
- **Multi-hop resource-acquisition planning** — nested-Interest recursion is invisible at the 4R grain; the OV's `PLC_Cycle` tracking holds it
- **Cross-actor pattern recognition** — the 4R frame holds one actor's diamond at a time; surfacing recurring patterns across actors needs the OV's `PLC_Pattern` discipline
- **Theorist-grounded tactical recommendations** — the Catalogue's tactic-by-tactic depth lives below the 4R abstraction

## Mode-aware use

**Consultant mode:** the consultant walks the principal through the four bases early (often in the SPHERE-OPEN session or shortly after). The four-level sophistication scale doubles as a diagnostic — listen to where the principal places themselves vs where the engagement's substantive work suggests they actually are. The deployable walk-through (with voice and pacing matched to executive register) lives in `4R-coaching-script.md`.

**Principal-self mode:** the operator IS the principal; they use 4R as a self-orientation aid. The walk-through is delivered by the AI in a no-false-confidence register — the AI should not flatter the principal into All-Star self-placement.
